About

Project: Beacon is a Minecraft community focused on creating large events, unique projects, and experiences that bring players together. Instead of being just another Minecraft server, Beacon is a place where ideas become projects and players help shape what happens next.

What is the goal?

The goal of Project: Beacon is to create memorable Minecraft experiences with a strong community behind them. We want to create:

  • Large-scale projects
  • Unique events
  • A community of players and creators
  • Worlds shaped by the people in them

The Community

Project: Beacon is built by everyone involved. Players, builders, creators, and staff all have a part in helping the project grow. Whether you want to participate, help create something, or just follow along, you are part of the journey.

What comes next?

Beacon is just getting started. As the community grows, more projects, events, and ideas will be introduced. Every new project helps build the future of Project: Beacon.

Join the journey — follow updates, participate in projects, and help build something bigger.

The Earth Event Is Back

Hosted by bogdanyell, alongside the Beacon team

The 250-player Earth Civilization Event is officially being revived. Total freedom to build, conquer, and make history across a massive map of the Earth.

What 250 players · 5 continents · 1 Earth map
When 10 August, 3:30 PM (CEST)
How to join Submit an application

Your application should answer:

  1. Why are you applying to this event?
  2. What continent would you like to be on?
  3. What type of player would you say you are?
  4. What is your goal for this event? (optional)

If a continent already has too many members, applications for it will be rejected — please check availability before you submit.

See the Applications page for how to apply.

This is currently planned as Beacon's one and only season for now.
